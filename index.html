How we calculate this

1. Faster response & follow-up. Instant replies and automated follow-up typically lift lead conversion. We assume a relative increase in your conversion rate based on how quickly you reply today — the slower you reply now, the bigger the gain.

2. Reactivating old leads. We assume just of your old leads and past customers buy again over a year once they’re re-engaged by text.

3. Fewer no-shows. Automatic confirmations and reminders typically cut no-shows. We assume a reduction, then apply your close rate and average customer value to the extra appointments that now show up.

These are deliberately cautious assumptions — a realistic starting point, not a best-case promise.
